STARFALL UNITY REALISM INTEGRATION — ATTRIBUTION

Integration changes: converted selected assets to FBX and Unity PBR textures; RGBA metal/smoothness packing, AO channel conversion, runtime visual bindings. Ancient dragon now has 114 thick overlapping armour shields, six curved horns, 24 wing tendons, authored procedural scale maps and one combined skinned mesh with three material slots. Rig helpers removed; original rock LODs retained. No commercial paid assets were purchased.
The exploration suit resource remains credited, but its maintenance display has been removed from gameplay.

Art-fix revision: removed 24 malformed added wing tendons; retained original wing skeleton/membranes, 114 armour shields and six horns. Added calibrated head/neck motion and visual ground adjustment. Procedural antenna and fabricated structure meshes authored in the project, reusing existing ambientCG CC0 Metal010. NASA antenna photographs used only as structural reference, no NASA antenna mesh or photograph embedded.
